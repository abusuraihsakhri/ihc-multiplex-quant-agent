Multiplex IHC cell-count quantification

Compute descriptive PD-L1 and immune-cell metrics from previously counted cells. All calculation happens in this browser tab; no data is uploaded.

Input counts and analyzed area

Use de-identified aggregate counts, not clinical records. The calculator does not perform segmentation, cell classification, staining calibration, or assay validation.

Calculated metrics

Submit the counts to display results and enable exports.

Methods and interpretation

CPS = min(100, 100 × (PD-L1-positive tumor cells + PD-L1-positive lymphocytes/macrophages) / viable tumor cells).

TPS = 100 × PD-L1-positive tumor cells / viable tumor cells. Densities use the entered analyzed area (mm²). CD8/FoxP3 ratio is undefined when FoxP3 count is zero.

These are descriptive research metrics. Clinical PD-L1 scoring requires tumor-specific, assay-specific criteria, appropriate controls and expert review. No therapeutic eligibility is inferred.
